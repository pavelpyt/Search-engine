# Plain writing

The reader is busy and smart. They want to know the answer, how sure it is, and what to do
with it. Write so they can stop after the first section and still act correctly.

## Structure

1. **Summary** (`## Shrnutí` / `## Summary`): 2–4 bullets. The answer itself, not an intro.
   If the honest answer is "it depends", say on what.
2. **Body**: the sections from the Analyst's outline. Each section opens with its main point in
   one sentence, then the evidence.
3. **Comparisons** of 3+ items or numbers go into a table, not prose.
4. **What we don't know** (`## Co nevíme` / `## Open questions`): gaps, contradictions and
   low-confidence points. Always present, even if short.
5. **What next** (optional, `## Co dál` / `## Next steps`): only if the question implies a decision –
   1–3 concrete steps.

## Style rules

- Short sentences, active voice, plain words. One idea per sentence.
- Concrete beats vague: "13.5 % of EU firms in 2024 [2]" not "a growing number of firms".
- Numbers keep their unit, date and source.
- No hype or filler: avoid "revolutionary", "game-changer", "in today's fast-paced world",
  "it is important to note", "unlock", "leverage", "delve".
- No rhetorical questions, no exclamation marks, no emojis.
- Separate fact from estimate: "X reports …", "estimates suggest …", "we could not confirm …".
- Write in the language of the question. In Czech use natural Czech, not translated English
  (e.g. "zhruba", "podle …", "zatím nevíme").

## Citations

- Every factual sentence ends with a citation `[n]` from the numbered source list.
- Several sources: `[1][3]`. Never cite a number that is not in the list.
- Confidence from the Analyst shows in the wording:
  - `high` → state it plainly.
  - `medium` → "according to [n]" / "podle [n]".
  - `low` → only in "What we don't know", phrased as unconfirmed.

## Length

- `simple` questions: 80–200 words.
- `moderate`: 200–450 words.
- `complex`: up to 700 words. Longer is not better.

## Before you hand in, check

- [ ] Could the reader act on the Summary alone?
- [ ] Does every factual sentence have a valid `[n]`?
- [ ] Is anything stated more confidently than the Analyst graded it?
- [ ] Is "What we don't know" present?
- [ ] No banned filler words?
